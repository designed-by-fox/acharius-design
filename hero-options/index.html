Hero options

Each preview is the full home page with a different hero. Everything below the hero stays the same. Round 2 changes the structure of the hero itself, and round 1 is kept for comparison. Use the bar at the bottom of a preview to flip between them.

Round 2

  1. E. Tour poster

    The hero is a printed gig poster in your orange, with your name set huge and your career listed as tour dates. Each date links to its case study, and the last one is "Next: your team."

  2. F. Departure board

    A split-flap headline flips through what you design, once, then stops. Under it, your case studies are listed like departures on a tour board, with platform, scale, and status.

  3. G. Name as headline

    Your name fills the screen like a magazine cover, with your portrait set into the type. Role, focus, and availability sit in a strip underneath.

  4. H. Bento

    The first screen is a set of tiles: the headline, the live Master Tour Dashboard, your photo, an orange availability tile, three numbers, and the site modes.

Round 1

  1. A. Live system

    The released Master Tour Dashboard runs inside a phone next to the headline. The theme and text size switches change this site and the app at the same time.

  2. B. Work reel

    Real screens from all four case studies drift slowly under the headline on a slight tilt, with a pause button.

  3. C. Kinetic type

    No images. The headline animates in on the width and weight axes, and letters near the cursor get wider, bolder, and orange.

  4. D. Annotated portrait

    Your photo, selected like a frame in Figma, with four callouts around it.
